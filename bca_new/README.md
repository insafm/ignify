## 🏛️ Programme Overview & Academic Structure

The **BCA_NEW** curriculum under the National Education Policy (NEP) revision comprises **120 credits** distributed over 6 semesters, emphasizing computer science core principles, systems, development stacks, and mathematical reasoning:

| Evaluation Component | Weightage | Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Continuous Assessment (Assignments)** | **30%** | Minimum 40% required to qualify for Term-End Examination |
| **Term-End Examination (TEE)** | **70%** | Minimum 40% required in theory papers |
| **Combined Passing Standard** | **40%** | Separate passing in both assignment and TEE theory is mandatory |
