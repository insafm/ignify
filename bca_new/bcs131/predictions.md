# 🎯 High-Probability Exam Predictions

> **Synthesizing historical frequency, recency gaps across the last five sessions (Jun 2024–Jun 2026), and the June 2026 shift toward 10-mark bundled questions**, the 12 targets below represent the strongest candidates for the next BCS-131 / BCS-031 Term-End Examination.

---

## 📌 Structural Analysis & The June 2026 Format Shift

A major structural shift occurred in the **June 2026** paper. Whereas exams from June 2019 through December 2025 relied heavily on 4- and 5-mark short questions (eight 5-mark questions in compulsory Q1 and short notes in Q5), **June 2026 converted the entire paper into twelve 10-mark long-answer questions** (four in compulsory Q1, two each in Q2–Q5).

<div class="callout callout-important">

<strong>Preparation Rule:</strong> Prepare every high-frequency concept at <strong>10-mark depth</strong>—combining formal definitions, syntax rules, advantages/drawbacks, and complete compilable C++ code.

</div>
