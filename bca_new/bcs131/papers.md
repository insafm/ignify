# 📑 Previous-Year Exam Question Papers (Dec 2012 – Jun 2026)

> **Dataset Archive:** Complete collection of **30 original Term-End Examination (TEE) question paper PDFs** for BCS-131 / BCS-031 (Programming in C++). This primary archive forms the underlying dataset for all frequency analysis, canonical deduplication, topic rankings, and exam predictions on this portal.

---

## 🧭 Archive Overview & Structural Eras

Across **14 years (30 exam sessions)**, the examination pattern evolved across three distinct eras:

1. **Modern Era (2023 – 2026 • 8 Papers):**
   - Features the **June 2026 major format shift** from 5-mark short questions to twelve 10-mark bundled long-answer questions.
   - Dual-coded papers (`BCS-131 / BCS-031`) aligning old BCA with the new revised **BCA_NEW** syllabus.
   - Includes the online BCA programme (**BCAOL**) paper from June 2023.
2. **Intermediate Era (2018 – 2022 • 10 Papers):**
   - Consistent 8 × 5-mark compulsory Question 1 (40 marks) + four 20-mark choices in Section B (60 marks).
   - Includes supplementary paper `BCS-031(S)` from December 2018.
3. **Foundational Era (2012 – 2017 • 12 Papers):**
   - Baseline question rotation covering early C++ object models, procedural comparisons, and memory architectures.
   - Includes supplementary paper `BCS-031(S)` from December 2017 (Set-2).
