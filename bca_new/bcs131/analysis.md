## ⏳ Longitudinal Repetition Patterns Across Three Eras

Longitudinal tracking across three eras—**2012–2017** (12 papers, 1,440 marks), **2018–2022** (10 papers, 1,200 marks), and **2023–2026** including BCAOL (8 papers, 988 marks)—reveals clear shifts in examiner emphasis:

- **CLASSES** has held steady as the single largest mark source at **17.5%–20.1%** of total marks.
- **POLY_VIRT** climbed steadily from **12.6%** (2012–2017) to **13.8%** (2018–2022) and peaked at **17.5%** in 2023–2026.
- **TEMPL_STL** (8.9%–9.7%) and **CONSTR** (7.6%–10.7%) remained perennial anchors in every single paper session.
- **DEBUG** tracing questions appeared only in Dec 2017 and Dec 2017(S) (1.1% of marks) before disappearing entirely.
