## ⏳ Longitudinal Repetition Patterns Across Semesters

1. **The Mobile OS Dominance:** Examiners dedicate **at least 20–25 marks in every paper** to Mobile Operating Systems (iOS 4-layer stack, Android runtime/stack, iOS sandboxed directories, Android process lifecycle, and Binder IPC).
2. **Predictable Numericals Guarantee:** Every single examination contains at least **two 10-mark computational problems**:
   - **Page Replacement / Partition Allocation:** Present in 9 of 10 papers.
   - **Disk Scheduling (SSTF, SCAN, C-SCAN):** Present in 6 of 10 papers.
   - **CPU Scheduling (FCFS, SJF, SRTN, RR with quantum 2):** Present in 6 of 10 papers.
3. **Synchronization Classical Problems Rotation:** Examiners cycle systematically through Producer-Consumer, Dining Philosophers, Readers-Writers, Dekker's, and Lamport's algorithms.
4. **Linux Security & Architecture Consistency:** Linux questions appear in **9 of the 10 sessions**, making it an essential core preparation area.
