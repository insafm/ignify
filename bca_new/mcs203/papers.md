## 🧭 Archive Overview & Structural Trends

Across **5 academic years (10 exam sessions)**, the examination pattern of MCS-203 has remained remarkably disciplined and predictable:

- **Total Marks Offered:** 120 Marks per paper (Candidates attempt Question 1 for 40 marks + any 3 of Q2–Q5 for 20 marks each = 100 marks maximum).
- **Core Numericals Guarantee:** Every single paper features at least two 10-mark algorithmic numericals:
  - Either a **CPU Scheduling simulation** (FCFS, SJF, SRTN, RR) or a **Disk Scheduling calculation** (SSTF, SCAN, C-SCAN) in Question 1.
  - A **Page Replacement numerical** (LRU, Optimal, FIFO, LFU) or **Memory Partition Allocation** (First-Fit, Best-Fit, Worst-Fit) in Question 1 or Question 2.
- **Architectural Diagrams:** A mandatory 10-mark diagram question (iOS 4-layer stack, Android runtime stack, or Linux kernel architecture) appears in every session.
- **Concurrency & Deadlocks:** Question 1 or 2 systematically tests Coffman conditions, Havender's prevention rules, RAG graph cycles, or semaphore-based synchronization.
