## 🧭 Archive Overview & Structural Trends

Across **13 academic years (26 exam sessions)**, the examination pattern of BCS-040 has maintained a clear 50-mark blueprint:

- **Total Marks Offered:** 70–80 Marks per paper (Candidates attempt Section A: 4 of 5/6 questions × 5 marks = 20 marks, plus Section B: 3 of 4 questions × 10 marks = 30 marks $\implies$ 50 marks total).
- **Single-Click Compilation:** For convenient offline revision, all 26 papers have been merged in exact chronological order:
  - 📥 **[Download BCS-040 Combined All Papers (2013–2026) PDF](/bca_new/bcs040/pqp/BCS-040_Combined_2013-2026.pdf ':ignore')** *(129 Pages • 3.38 MB)*.
- **Section B Workhorses:** Every paper features substantial numerical algorithms in Section B:
  - **One-Way ANOVA table** testing treatment differences against $F_{\text{crit}}$.
  - **Time Series Moving Average** (3-period or 4-period centered) with trend plot.
  - **Chi-Square test** for $2 \times 2$ or $r \times c$ contingency table independence.
  - **Correlation and Linear Regression** equations ($Y = a + bX$).
