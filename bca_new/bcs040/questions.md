## 🏆 Topic Modules by Priority & Exam Weightage

### 🔴 Tier 1: Mandatory Mastery (Very High Priority • 76.5% of Total Exam Marks)

Mastering these six modules covers the vast majority of compulsory short-answer questions and recurring Section B anchors across all 26 exam sessions:

#### [Module 01: Sampling Techniques & Sampling Distributions](/bca_new/bcs040/topics/01-sampling-distributions.md)
* **Syllabus Units:** Block 2 Unit 4 & Block 4 Units 12–14
* **Historical Weight:** **56 Appearances** (Offered in 23 of 26 papers; 88.5%) • **Rank #1**
* **Canonical Questions:** **13 Unique Questions** (Q001 – Q013)
* **Core Themes Covered:**
  * Sample vs. census approach advantages and disadvantages
  * Simple Random Sampling (SWR vs. SWOR) and random numbers
  * Constructing sampling distribution of sample mean and verifying unbiasedness ($E(\bar{x}) = \mu$)
  * Stratified Random Sampling principles, proportional vs. optimum allocation
  * Linear systematic sampling vs. circular systematic sampling
  * Cluster sampling and multistage sampling designs
