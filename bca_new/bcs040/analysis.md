## 🔍 Key Longitudinal Trends & Examiner Patterns

1. **Parameter Recycling:** IGNOU examiners routinely recycle identical numerical contexts with small mutations. For example:
   - Flipping $\Sigma Y$ from $-6$ to $+6$ in regression problems.
   - Changing $\Sigma X^2$ from $61$ to $50$.
   - Shifting sample sizes from $n=20$ to $n=10$ while keeping the core outlier calculation identical.
2. **Two-Tailed Critical Value Trap:** Several hypothesis testing questions supply a two-tailed critical value (e.g., $1.96$) even when the problem narrative phrases a one-tailed directional claim (e.g., "test whether the new method increases yield"). Students must explicitly state the hypothesis direction and compare against the provided table value.
3. **Section B Stability:** Over 80% of all Section B questions across the 13-year dataset come from ANOVA, Moving Averages, Chi-Square Independence, and Linear Regression.
